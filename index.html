Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

In short: Vdit is an offline video editor. Your videos, photos, music and projects stay on your PC. The app does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal data, and it has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Windows app Vdit (video editor), published on the Microsoft Store by “C1” (“we”, “us”).

2. Information we collect

None. Vdit runs entirely on your computer. We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, device identifiers, location, usage statistics or crash reports, and we never see the media you edit.

3. Your files

Vdit opens only the files you choose or drag into it. Projects are saved on your PC (in your AppData folder) and exported videos are written to the folder you pick. Nothing is sent to any server. Uninstalling the app removes the app; your exported videos and project files remain yours to keep or delete.

4. Third-party services

The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or social SDKs. Video decoding and encoding use the FFmpeg libraries bundled inside the app. The Microsoft Store may process data when you download the app; that is covered by Microsoft’s Privacy Statement.

5. Data sharing, sale and retention

Because no data is collected, nothing is shared with, transferred to or sold to anyone, and nothing is retained on any server.

6. Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on GitHub, or use the support contact on the app’s Microsoft Store page.
